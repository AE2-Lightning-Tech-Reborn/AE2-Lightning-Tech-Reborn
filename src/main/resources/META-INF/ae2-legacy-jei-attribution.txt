AE2 legacy JEI conversion compatibility

The LT-local GtoIngredientConverter, GtoIngredientConverters,
GtoItemIngredientConverter and GtoFluidIngredientConverter adapt the conversion
protocol and behavior of Applied Energistics 2's IngredientConverter,
IngredientConverters, ItemIngredientConverter and FluidIngredientConverter.

Original project: Applied Energistics 2
Original authors: TeamAppliedEnergistics and AE2 contributors
Original source version: forge/v15.4.11

AE2 Lightning Tech changes: relocate the four types into LT's namespace and
scope the registry to the supported Useless GTO integration; preserve the
converter protocol, ingredient-type duplicate/fallback behavior, exact key/NBT
conversion and legacy display-count saturation. The bytecode adapter retains
Useless's original custom AEKey type, registration, rendering and bookmark
implementation. No code is installed into AE2's package or dependency JAR.
