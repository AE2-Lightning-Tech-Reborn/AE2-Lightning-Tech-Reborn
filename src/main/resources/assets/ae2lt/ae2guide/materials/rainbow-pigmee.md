# Rainbow Pigmee Coloring

Name a Pigmee `jeb_` and strike its dropped item or placed block with lightning to obtain a Rainbow Pigmee.

Craft one Rainbow Pigmee with one colorable item to advance that item through Minecraft's 16 dye colors. An uncolored item starts at white. This works in the 2×2 inventory grid and returns the Pigmee after crafting.

To choose a specific color, place the Pigmee in the center of a 3×3 crafting grid and replace the four Dye Bases in that color's dye recipe with four identical colorable items. The output contains four recolored items; the Pigmee remains.

Item names and other saved data survive recoloring. Four-item recoloring requires a stackable result. Supported families include vanilla colored items and complete 16-color item families from other mods. Uncolored variants are recognized from loaded dye recipes and rechecked after recipe reloads.
