# Overload Singularity & Ultimate Overload Core

<ItemGrid>
  <ItemIcon id="ae2lt:overload_singularity" />
  <ItemIcon id="ae2lt:ultimate_overload_core" />
</ItemGrid>

**Overload Singularity** and **Ultimate Overload Core** are the late-game intermediates behind end-tier components and modular equipment.

## Overload Singularity

<ItemImage id="ae2lt:overload_singularity" scale="2" float="left" />

The **Overload Singularity** is required for Ultimate Overload Cores, high-tier Lightning Storage Components, and late-game recipes. It is produced in the [Lightning Simulation Room](../machines/lightning-simulation-chamber.md) or [Overload Processing Factory](../machines/overload-processing-factory.md) using High Voltage Lightning and FE (check your recipe viewer for exact ingredients and costs).

## Ultimate Overload Core

<ItemImage id="ae2lt:ultimate_overload_core" scale="2" float="left" />

The **Ultimate Overload Core** is also produced in the Simulation Room or Processing Factory using Extreme High Voltage Lightning and FE (see your recipe viewer for exact ingredients). It is both a primary ingredient for the [Lightning Collapse Matrix](lightning-collapse-matrix.md) and the mandatory core component for [Celestweave](../celestweave.md) armor pieces and the [Electromagnetic Railgun](../electromagnetic-railgun.md), installed via the [Overload Device Workbench](../devices-index.md).

> When a Lightning Collapse Matrix is installed in the Simulation Room or Processing Factory, High Voltage Lightning can substitute for Extreme High Voltage Lightning at a **4:1** ratio.
