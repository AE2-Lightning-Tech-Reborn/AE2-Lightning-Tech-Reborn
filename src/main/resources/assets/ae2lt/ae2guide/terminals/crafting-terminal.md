# Tianshu Crafting Terminal

The terminal combines crafting, smithing, an overload alloy anvil, stonecutting and a cell workbench. Choose a tab beside the work area. Workstation inputs remain in the terminal when it closes; breaking the wired terminal drops its real inputs.

The anvil uses normal repair rules, Forge repair callbacks and experience costs. The overload alloy anvil does not wear out from use or falling.

The cell page edits the inserted cell's partition, upgrade cards and fuzzy mode. Scroll through the partition rows. Keep or clear the partition template when removing a cell with the copy-mode button.

JEI and EMI can fill crafting, smithing, stonecutting and anvil inputs from the ME network or player inventory. Hold Ctrl during transfer to request crafting for missing ingredients.

With AE2WTLib installed, the wireless terminal also provides equipment slots, terminal settings, magnet filters and a trash page. Leaving the trash page deletes its contents. Combining terminals preserves stored inputs, energy, upgrades and links; conflicting inventories or settings prevent the recipe.
