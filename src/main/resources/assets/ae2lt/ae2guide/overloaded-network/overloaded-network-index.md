# Overloaded Network

The **Overloaded Network** family expands AE2 for large-scale bases with high-capacity channel backbones, remote wireless networking, and high-throughput automation blocks.

<SubPages />

## Choosing the Right Tool

Devices in this section fall into distinct operational roles:

| Purpose | Core Blocks / Tools | Summary |
|---------|---------------------|---------|
| **High-Capacity Channels** | [Overloaded ME Controller](overloaded-controller.md), [Overloaded Cable](overloaded-cable.md) | Expands controller channel output and trunk capacity while retaining AE2 controller multiblock rules |
| **Remote ME Grids & Wireless Terminals** | [Wireless Network](wireless-network.md), [Overloaded Frequency Card](overloaded-frequency-card.md) | Frequency-based same-dimension or cross-dimension ME links, wireless terminal coverage, and direct Frequency Card linking |
| **Automation I/O & Transfer** | [Overloaded Pattern Provider](overloaded-pattern-provider.md), [Extended Overloaded Pattern Provider](extended-overloaded-pattern-provider.md), [Overloaded ME Interface](overloaded-interface.md), [Overloaded IO Port](overloaded-io-port.md) | Uses the **Overloaded Wireless Connect Tool** for same-dimension face links and high-speed cell transfers |

<OptionalSection modId="appflux">
### Wireless FE Distribution (Applied Flux)

With Applied Flux installed, ME-stored FE can be distributed through three paths: the [Overloaded Power Supply](overloaded-power-supply.md) (same-dimension clusters), the [Overloaded Frequency Card](overloaded-frequency-card.md) (cross-dimensional remote power), or Induction Cards installed inside Overloaded ME Interfaces and Overloaded Pattern Providers.
</OptionalSection>
